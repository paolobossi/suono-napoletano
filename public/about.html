Tarantella Napoletana

About the Music

The Tarantella Napoletana is one of the most famous folk dances from Southern Italy, particularly associated with the city of Naples. The name derives from the tarantula spider, as it was once believed that the frenzied dancing could cure the poisonous bite of the spider.

This lively, upbeat dance is characterized by its fast tempo in 6/8 time and is traditionally performed with tambourines, guitars, accordions, and mandolins. The Tarantella is a symbol of Neapolitan culture and an important part of celebrations and festivities in the region.

About Naples

Naples (Napoli in Italian) is the third-largest city in Italy and the capital of the Campania region. With its rich history dating back to the 2nd millennium BCE, Naples has been a cultural powerhouse throughout the centuries.

The city is famous for its vibrant street life, colorful architecture, world-class museums, and delicious cuisine, including being the birthplace of pizza. Naples is dominated by the nearby Mount Vesuvius and sits on the beautiful Bay of Naples, offering stunning views and a unique atmosphere that has inspired artists, musicians, and writers for generations.

How to Use the App

This simple application lets you experience the Tarantella Napoletana music by pressing and holding the central button. While holding, you'll see a traditional Neapolitan fan appear at the bottom of the screen, celebrating the rich cultural heritage of Naples.
